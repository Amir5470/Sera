The Idea Behind Sera

Sera wasn't built in a corporate office. It was built in a high school hallway.

We noticed that being a student in 2026 is digitally exhausting. You check one app for your grades, another for your schedule, a third to talk to your soccer team, and a fourth to find out when the pep rally starts.

Sera fixes the fragmentation. By using AI to understand your unique school schedule, we create a private, student-led digital home for your classes and clubs. No teachers, no ads, just your school, all in one place.

We built Sera for students, by a student. It's the app we wish we had when we were in school — and we're on a mission to make it available to every student in the world.
