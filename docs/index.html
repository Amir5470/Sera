The Problem

School communication
is broken.

Currently, students rely on a chaotic mix of apps that don't talk to each other. Information is fragmented, and connecting with the whole school is impossible.

  • ❌ Discord - disjointed, requires a server for every single class.
  • ❌ Saturn - schedules only, with no real communication layer.
  • ❌ Google Classroom - formal, not designed for student social use.
