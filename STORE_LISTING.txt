Title: Sera — Your School, All in One Place
Subtitle: Classes, clubs & school feed — student-led

Description:
Sera connects your entire school in one place — schedules, clubs, and the school feed, all student-led. Stop juggling apps and stay in the loop with the people who matter most.

- School-wide feed for announcements, events, and clubs.
